## General questions

### Why is Agora discontinuing Signaling 1.x?

Agora launched Signaling 2.x in January 2024 with significant enhancements and has now achieved complete feature parity with 1.x. By focusing on a single, advanced platform, Agora can deliver better innovation, support, and competitive features to all customers. This ensures you're positioned on our most future-proof technology.

### How much time do I have to migrate?

You have a full 12 months from July 15, 2025 to July 15, 2026. This provides ample time for planning, testing, and executing your migration at a pace that works with your development cycles.

### Will the current Signaling 1.x service be affected during the transition?

No. Your Signaling 1.x service will continue operating normally with full SLA support, bug fixes, and escalation handling throughout the entire 12-month transition period.

## Technical questions

### Are all Signaling 1.x features available in 2.x?

Yes, Signaling 2.x has achieved complete feature parity with 1.x and offers additional enhanced capabilities including improved performance, better reliability, advanced analytics, and modern developer tools.

### How complex is the migration process?

Agora provides migration documentation to make this process as smooth as possible. Most customers can complete migration with minimal or medium code changes. Our support team provides technical assistance throughout the process.  
[Migration Guide](/signaling/overview/migration-guide)

### Will there be any service interruption during migration?

No, you can run both platforms in parallel during your migration testing phase. The switch-over to Signaling 2.x can be planned and executed at your convenience within the 12-month window.

### What happens to existing integrations?

Signaling 2.x maintains compatibility for core functions. Agora's support team will help assess your specific integrations and provide guidance for any necessary updates.

## Support and service questions

### What support will be available during the transition?

You'll receive:
- Full SLA maintenance for Signaling 1.x (bug fixes, escalations)
- Support team assistance
- Migration documentation and tools

### Will Agora continue fixing bugs in Signaling 1.x?

Yes, Agora will continue addressing critical bugs and providing escalation support for Signaling 1.x throughout the 12-month transition period. However, no new features or platform expansions will be developed for 1.x.

### What happens if I don't migrate by July 2026?

After July 14, 2026, Signaling 1.x will reach End-of-Life, meaning no further bug fixes or maintenance will be provided. We strongly recommend completing your migration well before this date to ensure uninterrupted service.

## Pricing and business questions

### How will pricing change with Signaling 2.x?

Signaling 2.x offers flexible packaging options including both prepaid and pay-as-you-go models designed to accommodate different customer segments. Your account manager will work with you to identify the most suitable package based on your usage patterns and requirements.

### I have a custom pricing agreement. How will this be handled?

Customers with existing custom pricing structures or enterprise agreements can opt for individual reviews with the account team to ensure a smooth transition that considers your current contract terms and usage patterns. Agora is committed to working with you on a case-by-case basis.

### Will I see cost increases?

Pricing will depend on your specific usage patterns and chosen package. While some customers may see changes, the enhanced capabilities, improved performance, and future-proofing benefits of Signaling 2.x provide significant value. Agora also offers volume discounts for multi-year commitments.

## Business impact questions

### How will this affect my development roadmap?

The 12-month timeline is designed to accommodate your development cycles. Many customers find that migrating to Signaling 2.x actually accelerates their roadmap due to enhanced features and improved developer experience.

### What if I am in the middle of a major product launch?

The migration timeline is flexible within the 12-month window. Agora can work with your team to plan the migration around your product launch schedule and provide additional support during critical periods.

### Should I stop developing new features on Signaling 1.x?

You can continue developing on Signaling 1.x during the transition period. However, Agora recommends planning new feature development on Signaling 2.x to take advantage of enhanced capabilities and ensure future compatibility.
